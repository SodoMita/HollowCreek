Hollow Creek
A one-street horror of townsfolk and things that aren't.

October, 1893. The harvest was good. Then the church bell rang on its own, and not everyone who came home was people anymore.

Hollow Creek is a multiplayer social-deduction game squashed onto a single street. You can only walk left and right - but that is enough. Somewhere between the pumpkin patch and the grain silo, one or two of your neighbours are Hollow, and they are very good at pretending.

HOW A NIGHT GOES
- Everyone gets a lantern and a street to walk. Chores belong to the town, not to a person: harvest the pumpkins, draw water from the well, cover the open graves. Any living townsfolk can finish any chore, so killing someone never stops the work - it only stops them.
- Working costs breath. Tired hands work slower, and at zero nothing gets done at all. Stand still and breathe.
- Find a body and report it, or ring the bell yourself. Everyone then WALKS to the town square on their own two feet - no teleporting, no hiding in a corner while the town decides who hangs.
- The town votes. The condemned are not hanged here. They are locked in an iron cage in the square, still able to plead, still able to vote, still able to accuse you of exactly the wrong thing.
- A caged soul can be freed three ways: the town votes to release them, somebody finds the iron key hidden somewhere along the street, or the Hollow reaches them where they sit.
- The Hollow can kill, and can snuff every lantern in town at once. Crew win by finishing every chore, or by putting every Hollow behind bars or in the ground.

The lantern's reach is all you can see. Speech floats out of your head as a bubble, and only people close enough to see you can read it. The dead wander as ghosts and whisper only to each other.

Everything is written into the town chronicle - every step, chore, word, vote and kill - and it stays sealed until the night is over.

CONTROLS
A / D or arrow keys - walk
E - interact: report a body, take the key, free a prisoner, ring the bell
E (hold) - work a chore
Q or Space - your ability (kill if you are Hollow, ring the bell if you are not)
F - snuff the lights (Hollow only)
Enter - speak (your words appear above your head)
Touchscreen works too. Everything is playable on a phone.

SETTINGS
Open Settings from the menu, HUD, lobby, or end screen. Adjust master, music, ambience and effects volumes; adaptive music and the proximity heartbeat; render quality and resolution; leaves, fog, glow, vignette, flicker and reduced motion; HUD scale, opacity and optional panels; touch controls; and every keyboard binding. Chores can use hold or toggle input. Settings are saved locally in your browser.

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS
Open the game on any device, type a name, and found a town. Share the four-letter code. 4 to 10 players, best with 6 or more. One person should keep the page open as the host - whoever joined last holds the lantern and can start each night. Towns remember their people: come back to the same code and you take your old seat.

The game needs a server for the authoritative simulation, so it is honest about cheating: your client only ever sends "I am walking left". The server owns every position, every role, every cooldown and every outcome, and it only tells you what your lantern can see. A modified client cannot wallhack, cannot teleport, cannot see who is Hollow.

WHO IS HOLLOW IS ACTUALLY RANDOM
Roles are dealt with a Fisher-Yates shuffle over the cryptographic RNG, and we measured it rather than assumed it: across hundreds of real games every seat is equally likely to be the Hollow, regardless of who joined first. An earlier version used a sort-based "shuffle" that made the first joiner the Hollow 43% of the time. That is fixed and tested.

CREDITS
Everything - art, generative score, simulation - is procedural and hand-made. No audio files: wind, bowed bass, a recurring music-box theme, meeting pulses, role stings and the heartbeat are synthesised in your browser as you play. The score leans into danger, meetings and blackouts, with separate mixer controls for music, ambience and effects.

Hollow Creek is an online multiplayer game. It needs other people - ideally in the same room as you, shouting.
